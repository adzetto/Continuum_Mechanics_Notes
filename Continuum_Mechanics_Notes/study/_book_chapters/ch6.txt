Chapter 6
Balance Laws
We are now in a position to study the basic balance laws of continuum
physics. These are axioms concerning the nature of mass, momentum,
energy and entropy pertaining to arbitrary parts of a material body.
We are concerned with scalar and vector balance laws.

6.4
Euler’s postulates
Euler’s postulates apply to an arbitrary part S of a body. They entail
the fundamental assumption that the motion of the part is governed
by the same equations that govern the motion of a rigid body. Thus,
the rate of change of the linear momentum of a ﬁxed set of material
points is balanced by the net force acting on it, i.e.,
